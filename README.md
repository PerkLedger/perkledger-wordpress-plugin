# PerkLedger Loyalty Rewards & Digital Pass WordPress Plugin

Official B2B Loyalty Infrastructure Bridge for WordPress & WooCommerce. Seamlessly embed digital stamp passes, floating customer rewards widgets, and automatic partner perk banners into your WordPress site.

---

## Features

- **Floating Customer Launcher Widget:** Automatically embeds a customizable floating loyalty widget on your site.
- **Shortcode Support:** Place your store's loyalty pass anywhere using `[perkledger_pass]`.
- **WooCommerce Integration:** Automatically rewards registered shoppers upon order completion.
- **100% Brand Color Harmony:** Adapts dynamically to your merchant accent color and radius settings.
- **Multi-Tenant SaaS Engine:** Powered by the generic PerkLedger B2B Cloud Infrastructure.

---

## Installation

1. Download the latest `perkledger-rewards.zip` release.
2. Log into your WordPress Admin Dashboard.
3. Navigate to **Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin**.
4. Upload `perkledger-rewards.zip` and click **Activate**.
5. Go to **PerkLedger** menu in your WordPress sidebar, enter your **Merchant ID** (e.g., `funkychips`), and click **Save Settings**.

---

## Shortcodes

To display the customer loyalty pass inside any page, post, or elementor block:

```text
[perkledger_pass]
```
